- **What failed / surprised me:**
  - Landmine (fixed): after renaming folder + `dataset_info.name` to `libero_spatial_no_noops`, TFRecord shards must also be renamed `libero_spatial-train.*` → `libero_spatial_no_noops-train.*`, or TFDS raises NotFoundError.
  - Patched `finetune.py` to `attn_implementation="sdpa"` (no flash-attn on Blackwell).
  - Host reboot mid-train (~step 9006) killed the first run; relaunched from step 0.
  - **`finetune.py` defaults to `save_latest_checkpoint_only=True`** — each 5k save overwrites the previous one. The 25k–45k checkpoints were lost; only the manual 20k snapshot and the final 50k are available for checkpoint selection. For future arms, pass `--save_latest_checkpoint_only False` (≈15 G per checkpoint) or snapshot each save.
  - ~7.4 s/step → 50k steps ≈ 5 days wall-clock on one RTX PRO 6000.
